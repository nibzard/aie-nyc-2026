## What the timeline revealed (day two)

- The morning keynotes ran as published: Ramp, Sonar, Coinbase, OpenAI,
  Bloomberg, then the compute block (Compound, SemiAnalysis/SF Compute,
  Jump, Compute Exchange).
- The published 12:40–2:05 PM "Networking Break" again carried unlisted
  content: pre-recorded AIE online-track talks (Intuit, Jellyfish, Michele
  Grant, Nori Agentic) plus a Blackstone fireside.
- The CAIS accessibility talk ("Stop Wasting Tokens on Products People
  Can't Use", Jemima Abu) aired out of its breakout slot, around 4:11 PM ET.
- One agenda swap: the 11:30 AM main-stage slot ran Evan Conrad's "How
  Compute Markets Work" instead of Lincoln Murr's Coinbase talk, which
  stayed in its parallel room.
- The day closed with the Apollo fireside, Basis, and Runway keynotes, MC
  closing remarks, and an off-agenda Swyx send-off announcing AI Engineer
  Europe (London, February).
