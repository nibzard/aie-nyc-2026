## Speaker details
**Aparna Dhinakaran** - Co-Founder & Chief Product Officer, Arize AI. Aparna Dhinakaran co-founded Arize AI, a pioneer in AI and agent observability and evaluation, in 2020. She builds tools for understanding AI behavior and turning model and agent failures into engineering improvements, connecting observability with evaluations that distinguish a wrong answer from the routing, retrieval, tool-use, or context failure that caused it. Previously she was a machine learning engineer and leader at Uber, where she built core ML infrastructure platforms including Michelangelo, and she also worked at Apple and TubeMogul (acquired by Adobe). She holds a BS in EECS from UC Berkeley, is on a leave of absence from Cornell University's computer vision PhD program, is a Forbes 30 Under 30 honoree, and is a frequent speaker on LLM evaluation and AI observability. Dynatrace completed its acquisition of Arize AI on October 1, 2026.
