## Five themes of the day

1. **The diffusion gap: intelligence runs ahead of adoption.** [Anthropic](../01-sessions/006-anthropic-accelerating-frontier-ai-in-financial-services.md) opened with it - what a model can do on its own doubles every four months, while three quarters of AI's economic gains go to a fifth of companies - and named finance the next threshold crossing after coding and cyber. [Sid Pednekar](../01-sessions/038-everyone-has-ai-who-has-the-advantage-three-pillars-design.md) quantified the same gap from the buyer side: only about 37% of enterprises report positive EBIT contribution from AI, and only about 6% are high performers. The [MC's return segment](../01-sessions/042-mc-resumes-sponsor-acknowledgments-conference-reflections.md) gave it a ladder: 90% LLM rollout, 60% trained users, 50% coding agents, 5% non-engineering agents. The [Kepler fireside](../01-sessions/035-ai-and-the-future-of-fundamental-investing-kepler-fireside.md) added the human constraint - information still has to get into your head.
2. **Agents that run the business, not copilots beside it.** [Arca](../01-sessions/018-inside-arca-s-long-horizon-agents-that-run-the-business.md) gives agents whole 10-day client outcomes; agents there "prompt people more than people are prompting agents" and have done "90% of the work" before a person acts. [Bridgewater](../01-sessions/003-vault-making-unstructured-data-work-for-investing-bridgewater.md) treats agents as a second class of reader: 100-plus queries per second against about 100 per hour from humans, ending in an always-on investor workbench. [Range](../01-sessions/022-extra-lunch-talk-financial-planners-building-their-own-agents.md) pushed authorship to non-coders - 24 skills company-wide, 17 in planning, all written by planners, marketers, and salespeople. [Arize](../01-sessions/013-what-does-an-ai-engineer-do-build-the-loop-not-the-feature.md) framed the engineer's job as building that always-on loop.
3. **Govern the agent: authorization, controls, and side channels.** [Two Sigma](../01-sessions/008-balancing-acceleration-and-safety-for-ai-agents.md) opened with the $900 million Citi/Revlon wire error - three humans approved a correct warning - and answered with five composable controls instead of lockdown. The [Intent Based Auth panel](../01-sessions/020-intent-based-auth-panel-runtime-authorization-for-agents.md) and [Keycard's lethal-trifecta demo](../01-sessions/023-extra-lunch-talk-your-agent-is-a-lethal-trifecta-task-scoped.md) converged on the same fix: authorize each tool call against a mission, with ephemeral tokens that expire within minutes. [Stealthium](../01-sessions/025-extra-lunch-talk-what-a-gpu-co-tenant-can-learn-about-your.md) extended the threat model to hardware: a GPU co-tenant inferred model information from timing in the demonstrated setups. [Revolut](../01-sessions/036-shipping-like-a-startup-regulated-like-a-bank-self-hosting.md) described sandboxing and human review in its regulated engineering environment.
4. **Evals built on your own work history are the moat.** [Vals AI](../01-sessions/033-beyond-the-model-card-the-jagged-frontier-of-ai-in-finance.md) made the sharpest cost claim of the day: 10 to 12 engineers used a reported $1.5 million worth of free tokens in a month, and replaying GitHub pull-request history as agent tasks cut spend 90% for the same output. Vals has crossed six trillion tokens of evaluation, and warns that "agents solve the test, not the problem." [Coatue](../01-sessions/043-a-hedge-fund-as-a-function-coatue.md) agreed from the buy side: compartmentalize into units you can evaluate, then hill climb - without a North Star "you're just fidgeting around." [Southbridge](../01-sessions/028-extra-lunch-talk-no-universal-schema-just-in-time-ontologies.md) ran a statewide Ohio entity-resolution job for $795 against a projected $10,000, and [Bridgewater](../01-sessions/003-vault-making-unstructured-data-work-for-investing-bridgewater.md) benchmarked search with about 200 hand-labeled examples in under a week.
5. **Own the substrate: infrastructure economics and open foundations.** [Revolut](../01-sessions/036-shipping-like-a-startup-regulated-like-a-bank-self-hosting.md) self-hosts GLM on 56 B200 GPUs at 30 cents per million tokens - one-tenth of the previous Anthropic and OpenAI invoice - and reported 70% better lead time in an internal comparison. That comparison does not isolate the model change from other causes. [Modal](../01-sessions/031-great-agents-start-with-great-infrastructure-modal-sandboxes.md) set the infrastructure bar: brains separated from hands, 10,000 sandboxes per second, and a warning not to vibe-code a sandbox platform. [Perpetual](../01-sessions/029-extra-lunch-talk-reliable-and-intelligent-workflows-for-fina.md) designed for about 95% of model decisions never needing a frontier model, so local inference becomes a configuration change. [Wells Fargo](../01-sessions/016-wells-fargo-foundation-model-application-to-fraud-detection.md) trained its own 1-billion-parameter transaction model from scratch. [Nous Research](../01-sessions/049-hermes-gives-you-wings-build-agents-on-open-foundations-you.md) closed the loop: build agents on open foundations you own, because tomorrow's moats are network effects, reputation, and reliability.

## Practical takeaways for AI engineers

- Benchmark the system before you fix it. About 200 hand-labeled questions, built in under a week, turned a vague complaint into a metric - and five improvements moved precision at 10 from about 60% to 90-95%. ([003](../01-sessions/003-vault-making-unstructured-data-work-for-investing-bridgewater.md))
- Replay your own work history as evals. Treat each pull request as an issue-resolution pair, run agents against them, and learn which models are overkill. Vals cut spend 90% this way. Judge benchmarks on validity, reliability, and discriminability - not five or six vibe-check samples. ([033](../01-sessions/033-beyond-the-model-card-the-jagged-frontier-of-ai-in-finance.md))
- Authorize per tool call, not per agent. Use OAuth 2.0 token exchange so each call gets an ephemeral, task-scoped, attributable token that expires within minutes and is never stored. ([023](../01-sessions/023-extra-lunch-talk-your-agent-is-a-lethal-trifecta-task-scoped.md), [020](../01-sessions/020-intent-based-auth-panel-runtime-authorization-for-agents.md))
- Score agents by risk and route review proportionally. A ticket summarizer and a production deployment agent do not belong in one approval queue; one heavy queue breeds shadow IT. ([008](../01-sessions/008-balancing-acceleration-and-safety-for-ai-agents.md))
- Turn near-certain approvals into rules. If you would say yes 99.9999999% of the time, make it a deterministic fast path instead of a human prompt - consent fatigue is the failure mode. ([008](../01-sessions/008-balancing-acceleration-and-safety-for-ai-agents.md))
- Have domain experts narrate judgment, not steps, when they write skills. Test on five to ten real runs before anyone else sees it, and ship with an owner plus a reviewer who is not the author. ([022](../01-sessions/022-extra-lunch-talk-financial-planners-building-their-own-agents.md))
- Split translation from execution. One planner model call turns a request into a typed semantic graph; a compiler that never calls a model turns it into an immutable DAG. Make waits, approvals, and follow-ups first-class nodes, and fail closed. ([029](../01-sessions/029-extra-lunch-talk-reliable-and-intelligent-workflows-for-fina.md))
- Keep the authorization boundary outside the agent. Inject credentials the agent cannot see, control egress down to paths rather than domains, keep audit logs where the agent cannot reach them, and separate the supervising agent from the worker. ([031](../01-sessions/031-great-agents-start-with-great-infrastructure-modal-sandboxes.md), [020](../01-sessions/020-intent-based-auth-panel-runtime-authorization-for-agents.md), [023](../01-sessions/023-extra-lunch-talk-your-agent-is-a-lethal-trifecta-task-scoped.md))
- Treat shared GPU hardware as an attack surface. The demonstration inferred model and workload information from timing. Its headline percentages and individual experiments measure different outcomes. Do not place untrusted MIG slices next to sensitive models, and watch for one kernel relaunched thousands of times against the same 40 kilobytes. ([025](../01-sessions/025-extra-lunch-talk-what-a-gpu-co-tenant-can-learn-about-your.md))
- Tune serving before buying GPUs. With the fleet fixed at 56 B200s, Revolut moved throughput from about 3 to over 7 requests per second and cut time to first token from over 10 seconds to about 1.6 seconds. ([036](../01-sessions/036-shipping-like-a-startup-regulated-like-a-bank-self-hosting.md))
- Ask explicitly for product polish from coding agents. Auto-save, optimistic updates, undo, archiving, and URL state are patterns agents skip by default. End every feature request with end-to-end verification by actually running it. ([027](../01-sessions/027-extra-lunch-talk-become-your-company-s-saas-layer-principles.md))
- Trace the factory, not just the product. Instrument the coding agents behind your agents, and evaluate the evaluators against golden truth datasets before shipping a new judge. ([013](../01-sessions/013-what-does-an-ai-engineer-do-build-the-loop-not-the-feature.md))
