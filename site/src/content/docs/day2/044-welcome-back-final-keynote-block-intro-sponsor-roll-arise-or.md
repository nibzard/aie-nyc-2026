## TL;DR
- A welcome-back voiceover returns MC Ksenia Se to the stage for the last block of programming at 4:26 PM ET, after a music interstitial cut off the previous Coder talk.

- She pitches a do-it-yourself agent project: build an agent that lists speakers and companies from the conference program, checks careers pages, matches open roles, and collects published compensation ranges so you can update your ranking.
- She hands off to Michael Manapat (Rowspace) and Jack McDonald (Apollo Global Management) for "AI in practice: an inside look at Apollo's approach".

## Key lessons
1. Hiring is a primary draw at AI Engineer. The MC spent the day talking to speakers and participants and found that "one of the strongest incentives to come to this conference is hiring" [07:48:00 | 4:28:35 PM]; even some speakers are both hiring and looking for work [07:48:11 | 4:28:46 PM].
2. Her agent recipe is a four-step workflow: take the conference program and list all speakers with their companies [07:48:32 | 4:29:07 PM]; rank them by whose work and talk you found most interesting [07:48:41 | 4:29:17 PM]; send your agent to those companies' careers pages to match open positions to your experience and collect published compensation ranges [07:48:51 | 4:29:26 PM]; then look at your ranking again [07:49:03 | 4:29:39 PM].
3. Salary data may legitimately change your mind. If the salary changes your ranking, "that's fine. You are updating your beliefs based on the evidence." [07:49:07 - 07:49:11 | 4:29:42 - 4:29:47 PM].
4. Act on site while the context is fresh: go find the people you would like to work with and talk to them now, because you have heard their talks and that is a good conversation starter [07:49:15 | 4:29:51 PM].
5. Automate research, not outreach: "don't make your agents send out your resume automatically. That's basically bad karma now." [07:49:34 - 07:49:40 | 4:30:09 - 4:30:16 PM].
6. The sponsor structure of AI Engineer New York 2026: Arise as presenting sponsor; Oracle, Sonar, WorkOS, and Axa as platinum; then gold, silver, and supporting partners [07:47:07 - 07:47:46 | 4:27:43 - 4:28:21 PM].
