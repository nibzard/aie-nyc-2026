## Session narrative
The livestream had sat on a countdown holding slide with pre-show music for just over eight minutes (segment 000). At 8:56 AM ET an unseen show-open voice-over broke in: "Good morning, and welcome to AI Engineer New York 2026," framing the event as "big ideas, sharp perspectives, and plenty of signal cutting through the noise," and asked the room to welcome "AI Engineer's General Manager, Leah McBride" to the stage (the automated transcription rendered her first name as "Leah"; the official agenda and speaker record spell it "Lia").

McBride kept the welcome short and warm. She noted this is the third AI Engineer event in New York, after two in 2025, and offered a welcome "from myself and Swyx to everyone." Her core framing tied the city to the program: New York is one of very few places where big ideas scale fast, and specifically "where frontier AI comes to meet centuries-old global financial institutions." From that framing she previewed the week — tracks in finance, engineering leadership, evals, and LLM, plus named speakers Aparna Dhinakaran (Arize), Max Kanat-Alexander (Capital One), and Tariq Shakked (Sonar) — and thanked presenting sponsor Arize, pointing attendees to their expo team all week.

The practical guidance was about participation: lean into learning, lean into connecting, and speak to as many attendees, speakers, and sponsors as possible, because the room is full of people who want to share, challenge, and collaborate on ideas. She closed with a forward calendar — Summit in San Francisco in November, London in February, the World's Fair, and a couple of new events next year — and handed the stage to "your MC for the day, Managing Partner at 10X, Alex Lieberman." The segment's final transcribed line, "What's up, everyone?" at [00:10:30 | 8:58:21 AM], is Lieberman's first words and continues in the next session record (002), which begins at the same wall-clock moment.
