## Divergences from the published agenda

- **The lunch block replaced the Networking Break.** The 12:15-2:05 PM slot is the only agenda item with no matching segment on the stream. It carried the seven unlisted talks above plus the early Modal start. (timeline)
- **The afternoon ran 25-30 minutes ahead of the agenda from about 2 PM.** Modal started about 26 minutes early ([Modal](/sessions/031-great-agents-start-with-great-infrastructure-modal-sandboxes/), [032](/sessions/032-hold-after-modal-talk-ends-early-no-speech-or-ocr-for-10-5/)), the Revolut talk aired at 2:52 PM against a 3:20 PM slot ([Revolut](/sessions/036-shipping-like-a-startup-regulated-like-a-bank-self-hosting/), [037](/sessions/037-break-room-turnaround-in-simons-room-after-shipping-like-a/)), and Coatue's keynote aired at 4:02 PM against a 4:30 PM slot ([043](/sessions/043-a-hedge-fund-as-a-function-coatue/)).
- **The "after stream end" items actually aired.** The agenda annotated [Hermes gives you wings](/sessions/049-hermes-gives-you-wings-build-agents-on-open-foundations-you/) and [Closing Remarks](/sessions/051-closing-remarks-speaker-applause-sponsors-housekeeping-netwo/) as starting after the stream cut. Both aired, at 4:36 PM and 4:51 PM, because the day ran early. They are not gaps.
- **The stream cut early relative to the published day.** The broadcast ended at 4:53:42 PM ET, after applause following the closing remarks - well before the agenda's 5:25-5:30 PM Closing Remarks slot. ([052](/sessions/052-silence-after-final-sign-off-until-the-livestream-cuts-appla/))
- **Stream-only artifacts.** The feed opened with an eight-minute countdown hold ([000](/sessions/000-pre-show-countdown-hold-before-show-open-holding-slide/)) and held through long untranscribed changeovers between afternoon talks: 10.5 minutes after Modal ([032](/sessions/032-hold-after-modal-talk-ends-early-no-speech-or-ocr-for-10-5/)), 12 minutes after Vals ([034](/sessions/034-break-stage-changeover-after-vals-ai-evals-talk-12-min-no/)), 13.6 minutes after Revolut ([037](/sessions/037-break-room-turnaround-in-simons-room-after-shipping-like-a/)), 12.7 minutes before the MC returned ([039](/sessions/039-untranscribed-gap-after-afternoon-talk-before-mc-returns/)), and 10.4 minutes inside the welcome-back block ([041](/sessions/041-untranscribed-gap-inside-the-welcome-back-block-10-3-min/)). Treat the agenda as a prior, not a grid.
